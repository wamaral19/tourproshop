Ordering & Delivery

Order Processing & Delivery 

  • Orders placed on a weekday before 8:00 a.m. (EST) will generally ship that day. Orders placed after 8:00 a.m. (EST), including Overnight Delivery orders, will generally be processed and ship the next business day (M-F).

  • For orders placed on weekends or holidays, orders will begin processing on the next business day (M-F). We do not offer Saturday delivery. Holidays and peak sales time may alter your shipment time frame.

  • Please allow up to 3 business days from the order date for items with logos and/or personalization. 

  • We are pleased to offer free standard domestic shipping for orders of $150 or more. 

International

  • We do not ship internationally at this time. We hope to offer expanded international shipping in the future and apologize for any inconvenience.
